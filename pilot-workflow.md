# InvoicePro Controlled Pilot Workflow

## Purpose

> Run this controlled pilot with **three to five Bangladesh-based freelancers** before offering the product broadly. The objective is to test whether the workflow is understandable and whether users will pay for its invoicing and follow-up value—not to claim guaranteed revenue.

## Participant flow

| Step | Participant action | Expected InvoicePro outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Sign in | Opens InvoicePro and signs in | The workspace opens with an empty dashboard and clear profile setup prompt. |
| 2. Set up profile | Adds business name, contact information, an optional logo, and bKash, Nagad, Rocket, or bank transfer instructions | The profile is stored and becomes the payment-information source for public invoice links. |
| 3. Save a client | Adds a real client’s name and contact details | The client is saved and can be searched or reused without re-entering data. |
| 4. Create an invoice | Adds fixed-price or itemized BDT services, a due date, discount, and note | The invoice total is calculated in BDT and receives a unique public share token. |
| 5. Share it | Copies the prepared WhatsApp or email message and sends it through the freelancer’s normal channel | The message contains the public invoice link; copying from a draft moves the invoice to Sent. |
| 6. Client opens link | Client opens the link without creating an InvoicePro account | The invoice details, freelancer logo, contact details, and selected local payment instructions display publicly. A sent invoice records a Viewed status. |
| 7. Follow up | Freelancer opens the follow-up screen on or after the due date | Unpaid and overdue invoices show the outstanding BDT amount, overdue days, and a copyable reminder. |
| 8. Record payment | Freelancer records a partial or full local payment manually | Payment history updates, outstanding amount is recalculated, and the status becomes Partially paid or Paid. |
| 9. Subscription control | Platform owner confirms offline payment through bKash, Nagad, Rocket, or bank transfer | Owner uses the admin panel to activate or extend the user’s subscription manually. |

## Pilot review questions

Ask every participant whether they could complete the process using only a phone, whether the client link looked trustworthy, whether the prepared messages saved time, and which part felt confusing. Collect exact wording rather than only a numeric score. A useful early signal is whether the participant asks to keep using the tool and accepts a small locally paid subscription after the pilot.

## Operator safeguards

The pilot should use genuine participant information only after their consent. Do not create fake customer records, reviews, payments, or invoices for public display. Manual subscription activation should happen only after the owner independently confirms the local payment.
